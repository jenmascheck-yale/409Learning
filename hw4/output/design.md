# Design (Problem 10)

A classic collegiate storefront look, built on the Yale branding (Yale Blue `#00356B`, Garamond headings, Verdana body) with a restrained gold accent.

## What I changed

- **Crest + refined hero.** Added a Campus Customs shield crest, a centered hero framed with Yale-blue rules, an "Est. New Haven" eyebrow, and a gold underline under the headline. It sets a real-store tone the moment you land.
- **Type hierarchy.** Garamond headings with collegiate underlines on section titles; Verdana body with generous whitespace, so pages are easy to scan.
- **Gold accent.** A single restrained gold (`#b9972e`) on the hero rule, the price on the product page, and the chat header — enough to feel premium without clutter.
- **Subtle motion.** Soft fade-up as sections and cards appear, and gentle hover lifts on product cards and buttons. A `prefers-reduced-motion` rule turns all of it off for shoppers who ask for less motion.
- **Product presentation.** Cards get a clean image frame, a blue border on hover, and a shadow lift; the single-item page shows the product in a framed panel with the price underlined in gold.
- **Chat feel.** The chat button lifts on hover and the panel header carries the same gold rule, so the assistant feels part of the store, not bolted on.

## Why it should help customers stick around and buy

- A polished, trustworthy storefront makes shoppers comfortable spending money — a scrappy page makes them hesitate.
- Clear hierarchy and whitespace help them find and compare products faster, so they reach a buying decision sooner.
- Hover lifts and the framed detail page draw the eye to the product and its price, nudging clicks into the detail view where the "buy" decision happens.
- Consistent styling across pages, cards, and chat makes the brand feel real and memorable, which brings shoppers back.
